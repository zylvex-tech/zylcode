#   USER_GUIDE, DEVELOPER_GUIDE) plus uncommitted rewrites of tracked files. The
#   result was a baseline that could not reproduce on a clean checkout: 39 of its
#   50 entries went STALE the moment the guard ran against the repository rather
#   than against one developer's disk. Green locally, red in CI.
#
#   Those untracked reports are not published and are already quarantined by
#   docs/README_INDEX.md, so excluding them loses no real coverage. Tracked
#   content is the only content this guard can meaningfully police.
