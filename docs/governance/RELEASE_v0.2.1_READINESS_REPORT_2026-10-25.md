## C. Tauri Schema Investigation (3 modified files)

Three generated schema files were modified in the working tree (not authored in this session):

- `apps/zylcode-desktop/src-tauri/gen/schemas/acl-manifests.json`
- `apps/zylcode-desktop/src-tauri/gen/schemas/desktop-schema.json`
- `apps/zylcode-desktop/src-tauri/gen/schemas/windows-schema.json`

**What changed:** the diff adds 6 entries — `core:app:allow-exit` / `core:app:deny-exit` and `core:window:allow-set-fullscreen-on-monitor` / `core:window:deny-set-fullscreen-on-monitor` (with allow/deny pairs).

**Interpretation:** These are **generated metadata** produced by the Tauri CLI describing *available* commands/permissions. The actual granted permission surface lives in `capabilities/default.json`, which was reviewed and does **not** grant `core:app:allow-exit` or `core:window:allow-set-fullscreen-on-monitor`. Therefore these schema entries represent options, not an expansion of shipped privileges.

**Decision: KEEP.** Legitimate generated metadata; no permission escalation in the shipped configuration. (Re-running `tauri schema` will regenerate them identically.)
