An assistant can suggest code.
A platform has to show its work.
Most AI coding tools answer a question with a suggestion. They do not leave a trace of why they answered that way. The code is there, the diff is there, but the reasoning is not. And without the reasoning, a suggestion is not a result.
ZylCode. Local-first. Bring your own key. Every word it generates is recorded, every tool it calls is logged, and every result is held in an evidence ledger you can audit yourself.
The workbench is the surface of a real engineering workflow. Explorer, editor, source control, a terminal that runs real commands with real exit codes, and an agent that plans before it executes. Nothing here is simulated; the verification pipeline runs the actual test suite in an isolated worktree.
A mission is a plan before it is a diff. You describe an outcome, and ZylCode turns it into files and components and a verification path. Consequential changes wait for a human to approve them. Nobody auto-approves the hard parts.
The agent executes. It gathers context, writes a plan, and calls real tools against the repository. In the hero build we watch it run through a real failure, then repair it on the next pass. That is not a demo of a model being right. That is the platform showing its own work.
Here is how claims are graded. Recorded observation is logged verbatim from a real command. Derived results are computed from those recorded facts. A hypothesis is marked as a hypothesis, plausible but not proven. An unknown is stated as unknown, never invented. A failure is kept, with its cause and its recovery, in the record. Everything has a label, and nothing is disguised.
Verification is a gate, not a suggestion. The real test suite decides pass or fail, and it runs in an isolated worktree so it cannot reach outside the candidate it is judging. The record keeps the result, the ledger, and the integrity check. That is the difference between a report and an engineering record.
On the repository side, the index is the evidence. Six hundred and sixty-nine files, two thousand nine hundred and forty-nine symbols, eight packages. Every symbol is pulled from a real scan, and the navigation graph is built from parsed imports and references, each edge labelled Deterministic, Heuristic, or Unsupported.
The provider layer is a contracted integration, not a platform. You keep your own credentials. ZylCode does not call a model without a provider you have configured and funded. Every reply passes through a documented allowlist, a per-user daily limit, and loopback-only development access. The model comes and goes; the evidence stays.
Nothing here hides a failure. The terminal shows a real command with a real exit code. The mission record shows a failed rung with its cause and its recovery. That is the design: a platform that tells you when it is wrong, because that is the only way to trust what it gets right.

What works is measured. What is disputed is labelled. What is next is a line in the roadmap. ZylCode is not a chat window that answers questions. It is a workspace where a question becomes a plan, a plan becomes a change, and a change becomes evidence.
